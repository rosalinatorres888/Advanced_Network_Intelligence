Hub Keywords Identified

Top 10% of keywords control 45% of network connections, indicating strong centralization around core concepts like "management," "organizational," and "behavior."

Community Structure

12 distinct communities detected with modularity score of 0.73, revealing clear thematic clusters in the knowledge network.

Bridge Concepts

Keywords like "psychology" and "decision" serve as critical bridges between communities, with high betweenness centrality scores.

Network Density

Network density of 0.134 indicates moderate connectivity, suggesting opportunities for strengthening knowledge integration.

Scale-Free Properties

Degree distribution follows power law with exponent -2.1, characteristic of scale-free networks found in many real-world systems.
